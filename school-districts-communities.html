Northern Colorado School Districts & Community Guide

School boundaries in Northern Colorado rarely line up with city limits, so buyers often compare communities by district before comparing neighborhoods. This guide pairs each major school district with the towns it serves and a local area resource from Schwartz and Associates (SAA Homes), Coldwell Banker Realty — serving Larimer, Weld, and Boulder counties since 2001.

Larimer County

  • Fort Collins real estate agents — Poudre School District covers Fort Collins, Timnath, Wellington, and much of the northern county.
  • Loveland CO homes for sale — Thompson School District (R2-J) serves Loveland, Berthoud, and the surrounding rural reaches.
  • Timnath CO homes for sale — master-planned growth on the Larimer–Weld line, served by Poudre School District.

Weld County

  • Windsor CO homes for sale — Weld RE-4 serves Windsor and Severance and anchors a fast-growing family market.
  • Greeley real estate agent — Greeley-Evans School District 6 is the county's largest and pairs with the G-HOPE down payment program.
  • Johnstown CO real estate — Johnstown-Milliken RE-5J serves Johnstown and Milliken along the I-25 / US-34 crossroads.
  • Fort Lupton CO homes for sale — Weld Re-8 serves the US-85 corridor between Denver and Greeley.
  • Brighton CO real estate — School District 27J covers Brighton and the I-76 corridor for Denver-metro commuters.

Boulder County & St. Vrain Valley

  • Erie CO real estate — Erie sits at the Boulder–Weld line and is served by St. Vrain Valley Schools.
  • Carbon Valley CO homes for sale — Firestone, Frederick, and Dacono are St. Vrain Valley communities along the I-25 growth corridor.
  • Lyons CO homes for sale — St. Vrain Valley serves this Boulder County foothills town along the St. Vrain River.
  • Berthoud CO homes for sale — small-town appeal between Loveland and Longmont, served by Thompson School District.

Mountain & foothills communities

  • Estes Park CO homes for sale — Estes Park School District R-3 serves the gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park.
  • Red Feather Lakes cabins — a quieter mountain entry northwest of Fort Collins, in Poudre School District territory.
  • Bellvue CO acreage — acreage and Poudre Canyon living minutes from Fort Collins.

School boundaries shift with new construction, so confirm a specific address with the district directly. For homebuying programs, financing guides, and a free conversation with a local agent, start at the SAA Homes buyer resource hub.
