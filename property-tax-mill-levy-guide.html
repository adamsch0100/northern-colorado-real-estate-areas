Northern Colorado Property Tax & Mill Levy Guide

Two identical-priced homes a few miles apart can carry meaningfully different property tax bills, because the tax is a product of the county’s assessed value and the stack of local mill levies (schools, fire, library, water, metro districts) where the home sits. Understanding that split matters before you buy — and it shows up again when you market a home to the next owner. This guide, prepared by Schwartz and Associates (SAA Homes), Coldwell Banker Realty, explains the geography.

County lines, not city lines

  • Fort Collins homes for sale — Larimer County assessment, with a well-established set of in-city levies; close-in and newer subdivisions can differ on metro-district layers.
  • homes for sale in Loveland CO — spans Larimer County, so the same valuation logic applies, but district boundaries shift by neighborhood.
  • Windsor CO homes for sale — straddles Larimer and Weld counties, which is exactly why the same street can carry two different tax pictures.
  • homes for sale in Greeley CO — Weld County assessment and a larger urban levy stack, with west-side growth adding metro-district layers on newer builds.

Where the corridor and metro districts matter most

  • Timnath CO real estate — fast-growing master-planned build-out means newer homes frequently carry metro-district mill levies on top of county tax.
  • Johnstown CO real estate agents — also split across Larimer and Weld counties, so confirm the exact county and district before you budget.
  • Berthoud CO real estate — Larimer County with a small-town levy stack; newer annexations can add district layers.
  • Erie CO homes for sale — sits in both Boulder and Weld counties, so the tax picture depends on which side of the line the parcel falls.
  • Carbon Valley CO real estate — Firestone, Frederick, and Dacono are Weld County with metro districts common on new construction.
  • Fort Lupton CO homes for sale — Weld County value pricing where the levy stack is often lower than the metro counties.
  • Brighton CO homes for sale — Adams/Weld county edge with an eye on how new-build districts price into the monthly payment.

Your agent and lender should pull the actual tax record and district list for any specific address — the county assessor’s site is the source of truth.
