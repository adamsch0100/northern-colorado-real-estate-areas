Moving to Northern Colorado — A Buyer's City-by-City Relocation Guide

Relocating to Larimer, Weld, or Boulder county? Each Northern Colorado community has its own mix of schools, commute patterns, housing stock, and price points. Below are buyer-focused area guides maintained by Schwartz and Associates (SAA Homes), Coldwell Banker Realty — serving Northern Colorado since 2001.

Larimer County core markets

  • Fort Collins homes for sale — Colorado State University, Old Town, the Poudre River Trail, and Horsetooth Reservoir.
  • Loveland CO real estate — the Sweetheart City, Boyd Lake State Park, and the downtown sculpture tour.
  • Windsor CO real estate agents — Weld RE-4 schools, Water Valley, RainDance, and Windsor Lake.

Weld County value corridor

  • Greeley CO homes for sale — University of Northern Colorado, G-HOPE down payment help, and the region's broadest buyer pool.
  • Fort Lupton CO real estate — US-85 value corridor between Denver and Greeley.

I-25 corridor growth towns

  • Timnath CO real estate — master-planned new construction on the Larimer–Weld line.
  • Johnstown CO real estate — historic Main Street at the I-25 / US-34 crossroads.
  • Berthoud CO homes for sale — small-town appeal between Loveland and Longmont.
  • Carbon Valley CO real estate — Firestone, Frederick, and Dacono along the I-25 growth corridor.
  • Erie CO real estate — Boulder–Weld county line growth with strong schools.
  • Brighton CO real estate — I-76 corridor value for Denver-metro commuters.

Foothills & mountain communities

  • Estes Park homes for sale — gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park.
  • Red Feather Lakes CO real estate — affordable mountain cabins and lake living.
  • Lyons CO real estate — Boulder County foothills along the St. Vrain River.
  • Bellvue CO real estate — acreage and river lifestyle at the mouth of the Poudre Canyon.
