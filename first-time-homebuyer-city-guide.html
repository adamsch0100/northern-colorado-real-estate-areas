First-Time Homebuyer's City-by-City Guide to Northern Colorado

Buying a first home in Northern Colorado usually starts with two questions: how far can the budget stretch in each town, and which down payment programs apply. This guide pairs each market with a local resource from Schwartz and Associates (SAA Homes), Coldwell Banker Realty — serving Larimer, Weld, and Boulder counties since 2001.

Qualified first-time buyers in Colorado can access CHFA down payment assistance to help bridge the gap to a first home. Program limits and terms are set by CHFA, so confirm current details with a licensed lender and a local agent before writing an offer.

Larimer County core markets

  • First home in Fort Collins, CO — CSU-area condos and east-side townhomes are common entry points.
  • First-time homebuyer Loveland CO — mid-corridor pricing with mature east-side blocks and newer west-side build-out.
  • Windsor CO homes for sale — Weld RE-4 schools draw first-buyer families to newer builds.

Weld County value markets

  • Greeley CO homes for sale — the region's most accessible price point, with UNC and G-HOPE program buyers.
  • Fort Lupton CO homes for sale — US-85 value for first buyers priced out of the core cities.
  • Johnstown CO real estate — small-town entry at the I-25 / US-34 crossroads.

I-25 & Boulder-county corridor

  • Timnath CO new construction — master-planned first homes on the Larimer–Weld line.
  • Erie CO real estate agents — St. Vrain Valley schools and newer neighborhoods for Denver/Boulder commuters.
  • Carbon Valley CO new homes — Firestone, Frederick, and Dacono entry-level new construction.
  • Brighton CO homes for sale — Denver-metro value along I-76.
  • Berthoud CO real estate — small inventory, first-buyer friendly between Loveland and Longmont.

Foothills & mountain

  • Estes Park CO homes for sale — mountain entry at the gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park.
  • Red Feather Lakes CO cabins — the region's most affordable mountain foothold.
  • Lyons CO real estate — Boulder County foothills along the St. Vrain River.
  • Bellvue CO acreage — Poudre Canyon living minutes from Fort Collins.

Down payment programs, first-time buyer grants, and lender incentives change frequently. Start with the SAA Homes buyer resource hub and a conversation with a local agent who can point you to current programs and current comps.
